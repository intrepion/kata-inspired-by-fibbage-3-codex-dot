# Velvet Bluff

A lounge where players invent plausible completions for unusual facts and try to find the real answer.

## Language

**Host display**: The shared screen introducing prompts and revealing results. It is not a player seat.

**Controller**: A private player view for bluffing, voting and applause.

**Bluff**: A deliberately false completion submitted by a player. Identical normalized bluffs have multiple authors.

**Applause**: One optional favorite answer per player per question, independent of the truth vote.

**Double Take**: The final question with two separate blanks, two bluffs and two truth votes.

**House decoy**: A prewritten false answer filling a short ballot; no player earns deception credit from it.
